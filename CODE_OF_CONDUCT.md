# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Everyone interacting with this project, in the repository, issues, reviews, or any related channel, is expected to act professionally and respectfully, to focus criticism on code and ideas rather than people, to accept constructive review, and to protect the confidentiality of any personal or client data this project handles.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publication of private information without permission, and any disclosure of personal or sensitive data processed by the system are not tolerated.

## Data confidentiality

This project processes personal and sensitive data of a law firm's clients. No real data, document, credential, or case detail may appear in issues, commits, pull requests, examples, or discussions, ever.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in removal of contributions, revocation of access, and, where applicable, legal measures. Reports are received in confidence at davcavalcante@proton.me and will be reviewed by the project owner.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces and to any public space where an individual represents the project.
